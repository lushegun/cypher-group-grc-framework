## 1. Purpose

This policy sets out how [COMPANY SHORT NAME] systems, devices, accounts and
information may and may not be used. It exists to protect [WHAT THE COMPANY MOST
NEEDS TO PROTECT, e.g. customer data, intellectual property, service
availability] and to make expectations clear to every individual.

This policy also serves as formal notice that use of [COMPANY SHORT NAME] systems
is monitored, as described in section 4.10.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to:

- All [LIST THE PERSONNEL CATEGORIES, e.g. employees, directors, contractors, consultants, interns and temporary staff] of [COMPANY LEGAL NAME], referred to collectively as "personnel".
- All [COMPANY SHORT NAME] accounts, including [LIST YOUR CORE SYSTEMS, e.g. email platform, chat platform, code repository, cloud provider, payment platform].
- All devices used to access [COMPANY SHORT NAME] information, whether company-owned or personally owned.
- All [COMPANY SHORT NAME] information in any form, including electronic files, printed material and verbal communication.

**Exclusions.** [STATE WHAT IS OUT OF SCOPE, e.g. personal use of personal devices where no company information or account is involved.]

## 4. Policy statements

> Drafting rule: every statement below uses **shall**, **must** or **shall not**.
> Never use "should" for a mandatory requirement. Every statement should be
> testable — an auditor must be able to design a test from the sentence alone.

### 4.1 General use

4.1.1 Personnel **shall** use [COMPANY SHORT NAME] systems and information primarily for legitimate business purposes.

4.1.2 [STATE YOUR PERSONAL USE POSITION — e.g. "Limited, reasonable personal use of email, chat and internet access is permitted, provided it does not interfere with work, consume significant resources, or breach any statement in this policy." OR "Personal use is not permitted."]

4.1.3 Personnel **shall not** assume privacy in any material created, stored or transmitted on [COMPANY SHORT NAME] systems.

### 4.2 Prohibited activities

4.2.1 Personnel **shall not** use [COMPANY SHORT NAME] systems to access, store, create or transmit material that is unlawful, harassing, discriminatory, defamatory or obscene.

4.2.2 Personnel **shall not** attempt to gain access to any system, account or data for which they have not been granted authorisation.

4.2.3 Personnel **shall not** intentionally circumvent, disable or degrade any security control, including [LIST YOUR KEY CONTROLS, e.g. endpoint protection, disk encryption, logging, multi-factor authentication].

4.2.4 Personnel **shall not** use [COMPANY SHORT NAME] systems, information or customer relationships to operate a personal business or a competing venture.

4.2.5 [ADD ANY INDUSTRY-SPECIFIC PROHIBITION — e.g. regulated-data handling restrictions, trading restrictions, clinical-system restrictions.]

### 4.3 Credentials and accounts

4.3.1 Personnel **shall not** share their account credentials with any other person, including colleagues, contractors and line managers.

4.3.2 Personnel **shall** store all work credentials in [NAME YOUR APPROVED PASSWORD MANAGER OR "the company-provided password manager"] and **shall not** store credentials in browsers, spreadsheets, notes applications, chat messages or source code.

4.3.3 Detailed authentication requirements are set out in [REFERENCE YOUR PASSWORD AND MFA POLICY ID AND NAME].

### 4.4 Devices, including personally owned devices

4.4.1 Any device used to access [COMPANY SHORT NAME] information **shall** have full-disk encryption enabled.

4.4.2 Any such device **shall** lock automatically after no more than [NUMBER] minutes of inactivity.

4.4.3 Operating system and browser security updates **shall** be applied within [NUMBER] calendar days of release.

4.4.4 Personnel **shall not** access [COMPANY SHORT NAME] information from a jailbroken, rooted or otherwise modified device.

4.4.5 Personnel **shall not** permit any other individual to use a device while a [COMPANY SHORT NAME] account is signed in.

4.4.6 Personnel **shall** report a lost or stolen device under [REFERENCE YOUR INCIDENT REPORTING POLICY ID] within [NUMBER] hour(s).

### 4.5 Use of artificial intelligence tools

4.5.1 Personnel **shall** use only AI tools appearing on the [COMPANY SHORT NAME] approved AI tools list, maintained by [ROLE] at [LOCATION OF THE LIST].

4.5.2 Personnel **shall not** enter information classified [YOUR TWO HIGHEST CLASSIFICATION TIERS] into any AI tool that is not on the approved list. This expressly includes [LIST YOUR MOST SENSITIVE DATA TYPES].

4.5.3 Personnel **shall** treat AI-generated output as unverified and **shall not** use it in [PRODUCTION / CUSTOMER-FACING / PUBLISHED] contexts without human review by a competent person.

4.5.4 Requests to add a tool to the approved list **shall** be submitted to [ROLE], who will assess the vendor's data handling, retention and training practices before approval.

### 4.6 Software, services and shadow IT

4.6.1 Personnel **shall not** procure or begin using any third-party service that will store, process or transmit [COMPANY SHORT NAME] information without prior approval from [ROLE].

4.6.2 Personnel **shall not** install unlicensed software, or software from an untrusted source, on any device used for company work.

4.6.3 Personnel **shall** report any pre-existing unapproved service in use to [ROLE] without fear of penalty.

### 4.7 Communications conduct

4.7.1 Personnel **shall** verify recipients before sending any message containing [YOUR TWO HIGHEST CLASSIFICATION TIERS] information.

4.7.2 Personnel **shall not** discuss [COMPANY SHORT NAME] customers, incidents, unreleased work or internal disputes on public forums, social media or personal messaging applications.

4.7.3 Personnel **shall** forward suspected phishing messages to [YOUR REPORTING ADDRESS OR CHANNEL] and **shall not** interact with them.

### 4.8 Removable media and printing

4.8.1 Personnel **shall not** copy [YOUR TWO HIGHEST CLASSIFICATION TIERS] information onto removable media without prior written approval from [ROLE].

4.8.2 Printed material containing sensitive information **shall not** be left unattended and **shall** be destroyed by [DISPOSAL METHOD, e.g. cross-cut shredding] when no longer required.

### 4.9 Physical security of devices

4.9.1 Personnel **shall not** leave devices unattended in public spaces.

4.9.2 Personnel **shall not** place devices containing [COMPANY SHORT NAME] information into checked baggage.

4.9.3 Devices **shall** be locked whenever the user steps away from them.

4.9.4 [ADD ANY SITE-SPECIFIC PHYSICAL REQUIREMENT — e.g. clear desk policy, badge display, visitor escorting.]

### 4.10 Monitoring notice

4.10.1 [COMPANY SHORT NAME] **monitors** the use of its systems for security, availability and compliance purposes. Monitoring includes [LIST WHAT YOU ACTUALLY MONITOR, e.g. authentication logs, cloud activity logs, endpoint telemetry, email and file access metadata].

4.10.2 Monitoring is limited to what is necessary and proportionate. [COMPANY SHORT NAME] **does not** routinely read the content of personal messages. Content **may** be accessed where there is a documented security or legal requirement, approved by [ROLE].

4.10.3 By signing this policy, personnel acknowledge that this monitoring occurs.

> **Legal note when adapting:** monitoring notice requirements differ by
> jurisdiction. In the EU, EEA and UK, employee monitoring engages data
> protection law and may require a lawful basis, a privacy notice and, in some
> countries, works council consultation. Have [LEGAL COUNSEL / DPO] review this
> section before issuing.

### 4.11 Return of assets

4.11.1 On termination, personnel **shall** return all [COMPANY SHORT NAME] devices, access cards and printed material within [NUMBER] business days.

4.11.2 Personnel **shall not** retain, copy or transfer any [COMPANY SHORT NAME] information to a personal account, device or storage service at any time.

## 5. Enforcement

5.1 Violations may result in suspension of system access, formal disciplinary
action up to and including termination of employment or contract, and, where the
conduct is unlawful, referral to law enforcement.

5.2 Access suspension pending investigation is a protective measure and is not in
itself a disciplinary sanction.

5.3 Personnel who report their own accidental violation promptly and in good
faith will be treated as having acted responsibly.

5.4 **Exceptions.** Any requirement may be excepted only by written approval of
[ROLE], recorded in the [NAME OF YOUR EXCEPTION REGISTER] with a business
justification, a compensating control, a named accepting owner and an expiry date
not exceeding [NUMBER] months.
